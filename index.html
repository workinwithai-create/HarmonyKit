Can't figure out the harmony? Bring your lead and your chords. HarmonyKit writes singable harmony parts for it, plays each one alone on real choir samples so you can learn it, then scores every take you sing, bar by bar.

iPhone/iPad: flip the silent switch off (or use headphones). iOS mutes web audio when the ringer is silenced. Exports open the share sheet: pick "Save to Files".
